# LumiBot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026

## About LumiBot

LumiBot is operated by Team Luminescent for the Luminescent Platinum Discord community. It provides game-information commands, predefined support responses, and community moderation features.

## Information processed

LumiBot processes information supplied through Discord, including:

- Message text, links, and whether a message contains attachments;
- Discord user, server, channel, message, and role IDs;
- Usernames, server roles, and permissions;
- Slash-command options; and
- Limited timestamps and counts used to identify spam.

Message content is examined to provide requested support responses and enforce moderation rules, including administrator-configured prohibited phrases and rapid attachment-spam detection.

LumiBot does not store message bodies or attachments in its database or operational logs.

The bot stores only the server configuration required for its moderation features:

- Server IDs;
- Administrator-configured prohibited phrases or links;
- Administrator-configured emulator terms; and
- Role IDs exempted from specified moderation checks.

Limited user, channel, and timestamp information may be held temporarily in application memory for spam detection. It is not written to the database.

## How information is used

Information is processed to:

- Provide game-information and community-support responses;
- Verify command permissions and role hierarchy;
- Detect prohibited phrases, malicious links, and spam;
- Protect users from phishing, compromised accounts, scams, and inappropriate content;
- Perform moderator-requested bans, removals, timeouts, and unbans; and
- Maintain the security and reliability of the application.

LumiBot does not use Discord information for advertising, user profiling, or purposes unrelated to its community-support and moderation functions.

## Automated moderation and user choice

LumiBot uses rule-based automated moderation configured by server administrators. Matching messages may be deleted, and accounts responsible for repeated or rapid spam may be removed from the server.

Users cannot individually opt out of server-wide moderation because this would allow community safety rules to be bypassed. Administrators may exempt trusted roles from specified checks. Moderation decisions can be appealed to the server's human moderation team.

## Data storage and retention

- Message bodies and attachments are processed transiently and are not retained by LumiBot.
- Temporary anti-spam identifiers and timestamps are held only in application memory and are not stored as permanent records.
- Server configuration remains stored until removed by an authorized administrator or until it is no longer required.
- Limited technical error logs may be retained, but are not intentionally configured to contain message content.
- Discord may independently retain messages, interactions, moderation actions, and audit logs under its own privacy policy.

## Data sharing

LumiBot does not sell personal information or share it for advertising, marketing, or user profiling. Information is transmitted to Discord only as necessary to respond to commands and perform moderation actions. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users and the service.

## Machine learning and AI

LumiBot does not use message content or other Discord information to train, fine-tune, evaluate, or develop machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models.

## Privacy requests

LumiBot does not maintain user profiles or store message histories, so it will ordinarily hold no persistent user-specific information. Server administrators may request deletion of their server's stored configuration.

Privacy questions, applicable data requests, and reports concerning LumiBot may be submitted through the [LumiBot GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/TeamLumi/Lumibot/issues) or raised with the Team Luminescent Discord moderation team.

## Children

LumiBot is not intended for anyone below the minimum age required to use Discord in their jurisdiction.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when LumiBot's functionality or information handling changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
